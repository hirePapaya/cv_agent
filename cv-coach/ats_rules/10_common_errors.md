# Common Errors

## Rules
- Spelling and grammar mistakes — run a spell-check and proofread; typos in section headings or company names are especially damaging.
- Inconsistent date formats across entries (mixing "Jan 2022" with "01/2022" with "2022-01").
- Inconsistent tense — past roles in past tense, current role in present tense, consistently applied.
- Tables, columns, text boxes, or graphics used for layout, which break ATS text extraction.
- Headers/footers containing name, contact info, or page numbers that get stripped during parsing.
- Acronyms or jargon used without ever spelling out the full term once.
- Overly long resumes (3+ pages for non-academic/non-executive roles) or resumes padded with irrelevant content.
- Missing or broken hyperlinks (LinkedIn, portfolio) — verify every link resolves correctly.
- Using the same generic resume for every application instead of tailoring to each job description.

## Why It Matters
These errors are the most frequent reasons strong candidates get filtered out automatically or dismissed quickly by recruiters, independent of actual qualifications.

## How to Apply
Run through this list as a final pass after all other checks — these are quick wins that catch issues other rule categories might miss.
