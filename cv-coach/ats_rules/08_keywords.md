# Keywords

## Rules
- Mirror key terms from the job description (titles, tools, certifications, methodologies) wherever the candidate genuinely has that experience.
- Include both the spelled-out term and its acronym at least once (e.g., "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)") so both forms match.
- Distribute keywords naturally across Summary, Experience, and Skills — avoid a single "keyword dump" block.
- Never include keywords for skills the candidate doesn't actually have ("keyword stuffing") — this fails human review and can backfire in interviews.
- Match the seniority/title language of the posting when accurate (e.g., if applying for "Senior Backend Engineer" and the candidate's title is equivalent, align the wording).
- Use industry-standard terminology over internal/company-specific jargon from previous employers.

## Why It Matters
Most ATS rank or filter candidates based on keyword overlap with the job description. Natural, honest keyword alignment improves ranking without triggering spam-detection or misrepresenting the candidate.

## How to Apply
Compare the resume against the target job description; flag missing high-relevance keywords the candidate has experience with, and flag any keyword stuffing or unsupported claims.
