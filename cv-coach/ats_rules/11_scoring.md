# Scoring

## Rules
- Score the resume against the target job description on a 0–100 scale, combining:
  - **Keyword match (40%)** — overlap of required/preferred skills, tools, and titles from the job description present in the resume.
  - **Format compliance (20%)** — adherence to file format, headings, fonts, and layout rules (sections 01–04).
  - **Content quality (25%)** — quantified achievements, action verbs, tailored summary, no common errors (sections 05, 09, 10).
  - **Completeness (15%)** — all expected sections present (contact, summary, experience, education, skills) with no gaps.
- Provide a score breakdown by category, not just a single number, so the candidate knows what to improve.
- Flag any single critical issue (e.g., unparsable file format, missing contact info) as a "blocker" regardless of overall score — these can zero out ATS visibility even with a high keyword match.
- Re-score after each round of edits to track improvement.

## Why It Matters
A single aggregate score hides *why* a resume underperforms. A category breakdown lets the candidate prioritize fixes — e.g., a 90/100 resume with a "blocker" format issue may pass 0% of real ATS filters.

## How to Apply
After applying rules from sections 01–10, compute the weighted score, list any blockers separately, and present both the overall score and per-category breakdown to the user.
