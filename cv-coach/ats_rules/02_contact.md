# Contact Information

## Rules
- Place contact details at the very top of the document, in the body text — not in a header/footer.
- Required fields: full name, phone number, email address, city + state/country (no full street address needed).
- Optional fields: LinkedIn URL, GitHub/portfolio URL, personal website.
- Use a professional email address (e.g., `firstname.lastname@email.com`) — avoid nicknames or numbers that look unprofessional.
- Write URLs as plain text (`linkedin.com/in/username`), not as hyperlinked icons or images.
- Do not include a photo, date of birth, marital status, or national ID — many ATS and EEOC guidelines flag these as risky or irrelevant.
- Phone numbers should use a consistent, standard format (e.g., `+1 (555) 123-4567`).

## Why It Matters
Contact info is the first thing both the ATS and the recruiter look for. If it's unparsable or missing, the candidate cannot be reached even if the rest of the resume is a strong match.

## How to Apply
Check that name, phone, and email are present as plain text near the top of the document, and that no contact info relies on icons, images, or header/footer placement.
