# Work Experience

## Rules
- List roles in reverse-chronological order (most recent first).
- For each role include: company name, job title, location, and start/end dates (Month Year — Month Year, or "Present").
- Use a consistent date format across all entries (e.g., "Jan 2022 — Mar 2024").
- Describe responsibilities and achievements as bullet points, not paragraphs — 3–6 bullets per role is typical.
- Start each bullet with a strong action verb (e.g., "Architected", "Led", "Reduced", "Implemented") — avoid starting with "Responsible for".
- Quantify impact wherever possible (%, $, time saved, scale of users/data).
- Avoid first-person pronouns ("I", "my") — resumes use implied first person.
- Do not leave unexplained employment gaps longer than a few months without addressing them (e.g., via a brief note or by adjusting date granularity).

## Why It Matters
Work experience is the primary section ATS keyword-matching and recruiters scan for relevant titles, skills, and tenure. Clear structure and quantified achievements make matches easier and demonstrate impact.

## How to Apply
Check reverse-chronological order, consistent date formatting, bullet (not paragraph) format, and presence of action verbs and quantified results in each entry.
