# Fonts & Typography

## Rules
- Use standard, widely-supported fonts: Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Georgia, Times New Roman, or similar.
- Avoid decorative, script, or custom-embedded fonts — if the font isn't installed on the parsing system, text may render as garbage characters or boxes.
- Body text: 10–12pt. Section headings: 12–14pt. Name: up to 16–18pt.
- Use bold and italics sparingly for emphasis — never rely on color alone to convey meaning.
- Avoid text effects: shadows, outlines, WordArt, underlines on body text (underlines can be misread as links).
- Maintain consistent font usage throughout — one or two font families maximum.

## Why It Matters
Unusual fonts and heavy styling increase the risk of text-extraction errors and make the document look unprofessional to a human reviewer if it does pass through.

## How to Apply
Flag any non-standard fonts, font sizes below 10pt or above 18pt for body content, and excessive use of bold/italic/underline/color.
