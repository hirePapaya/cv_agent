# Professional Summary

## Rules
- Place a 2–4 sentence summary directly below the contact information, before Work Experience.
- Lead with years of experience, role/title, and core domain expertise (e.g., "Senior Solutions & Cloud Architect with 5+ years...").
- Tailor the summary to the target role — reflect the title and top 2–3 priorities from the job description.
- Avoid generic filler phrases ("hard-working team player", "results-oriented professional") without supporting evidence.
- Write in third-person-implied style (no "I"/"my"), consistent with the rest of the resume.
- Skip an "Objective" statement (what the candidate wants) in favor of a "Summary" (what the candidate offers).

## Why It Matters
The summary is prime real estate — it's the first text both the ATS and a human reader see. A tailored, keyword-rich summary frames the rest of the resume and improves early matching.

## How to Apply
Check that a summary exists, is tailored (not generic boilerplate), is free of "Objective"-style language, and reflects the target role's title/keywords.
