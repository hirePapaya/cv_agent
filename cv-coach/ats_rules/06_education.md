# Education

## Rules
- List degrees in reverse-chronological order (most recent first).
- For each entry include: institution name, degree and field of study, location, and graduation date (or expected date).
- Omit graduation dates only if more than 15–20 years in the past and not relevant (to avoid age bias) — otherwise include them.
- Include GPA only if 3.5/4.0 or higher and the candidate is early-career (within ~3 years of graduation).
- Relevant coursework, honors, or thesis topics may be listed as a sub-bullet if directly relevant to the target role.
- Use the same date format as the Work Experience section for consistency.
- Certifications can live in this section or their own "Certifications" section — be consistent across the resume.

## Why It Matters
Education is a common screening filter (degree requirements, field of study). Consistent, complete entries ensure the ATS can match against minimum-qualification filters.

## How to Apply
Confirm institution, degree, and dates are present and formatted consistently with the rest of the document, and that GPA/coursework are only included when they add value.
